Dental Services

At Lipon Dental Clinic, we offer a comprehensive range of dental services to meet all your oral health needs. Our experienced team, led by Dr. Margie Payno-Lipon, is dedicated to providing high-quality care in a comfortable and welcoming environment. Whether you need a routine check-up or advanced dental treatments, we are here to help you achieve a healthy and beautiful smile.

Dental Services Offered

  • DENTAL CHECK-UP ▼
    Regular examination of your teeth and gums to detect cavities, plaque buildup, and other oral health concerns early.
  • TOOTH EXTRACTION
    800-1000     ▼
    Safe removal of damaged, decayed, or problematic teeth to relieve pain and prevent further complications.
  • ORAL PROPHYLAXIS (CLEANING)
    800-1500     ▼
    Professional cleaning of teeth to remove plaque and tartar, promoting healthy gums and fresh breath.
  • TEETH WHITENING ▼
    Safe and effective teeth whitening treatments to brighten your smile and boost your confidence.
  • DENTAL FILLINGS (PASTA)
    800-1500     ▼
    Restorative treatment to repair damaged teeth and restore their function and appearance.
  • COMPLETE AND PARTIAL DENTURE ▼
    Custom-made prosthetic devices to replace missing teeth and restore your smile.
  • CROWN AND BRIDGES ▼
    Restorative treatments to cover damaged teeth or fill gaps left by missing teeth.
  • ORTHODONTICS (DENTAL BRACES) ▼
    Correction of misaligned teeth and jaw issues through orthodontic treatment.
  • COSMETIC DENTISTRY ▼
    Procedures focused on improving the appearance of your smile and overall oral aesthetics.
  • ROOT CANAL TREATMENT ▼
    Procedure to remove infected or inflamed pulp from inside the tooth and save the tooth from extraction.
  • TMJ TREATMENT ▼
    Treatment for temporomandibular joint (TMJ) disorders to alleviate pain and improve jaw function.
  • SURGICAL REMOVAL OF WISDOM TOOTH ▼
    Procedure to remove impacted or problematic wisdom teeth to prevent complications.
  • PERIAPICAL DENTAL X-RAY ▼
    Imaging technique used to visualize the roots and surrounding tissues of teeth.
