Warm, Professional Service

At Lipon Dental Clinic, we believe dentistry is about more than just teeth—it's about people. Led by Dr. Margie Payno-Lipon, our team is committed to making every visit comfortable.

Our philosophy, often cited in our clinic, highlights that a dentist must be a dedicated doctor, an engineer to ensure function, and an artist to craft a beautiful smile.

Meet the Doctor

Background of the Doctor

Dr. Margie Lipon is a dedicated dental professional with 23 years of experience in the field of dentistry. She graduated from the University of the East and has continuously provided quality dental care with a commitment to patient comfort and excellence. Through years of practice and continuing professional development, Dr. Lipon has helped patients achieve healthier smiles and improved dental confidence through personalized and modern dental treatments.
